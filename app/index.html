How this works

  1. Parse the combat log once into a shared event store.
  2. Calculate combat results and independently check the important totals.
  3. Reconstruct team-debuff applications, refreshes, and target states.
  4. Compare debuff windows with matched clean-hit damage when enough samples exist.
