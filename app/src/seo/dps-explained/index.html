Combat metrics

DPS and Active DPS, in simple words

Two players can deal the same damage and still have different DPS. The difference is the clock used underneath the damage total.

Double checked. Kept local.

Damage

Damage is the total counted Physical damage in the selected session or fight. It is the number used by both DPS calculations.

DPS

DPS means damage per second. Strikeglass divides a player’s counted damage by the elapsed time from that player’s first counted hit to their last counted hit in the selected scope.

Long pauses between those hits remain part of this elapsed clock.

DPS = counted damage ÷ elapsed player time

Active DPS

Active DPS uses the same damage total but removes qualifying idle gaps from the combat clock. Strikeglass currently treats gaps longer than five seconds as inactive time when building this clock.

That is why Active DPS can be higher than DPS. If there are no qualifying idle gaps, the two values can legitimately be the same.

Active DPS = counted damage ÷ active combat time

Group share

Group share is the percentage of the selected group damage contributed by one player. It answers how much of the group’s damage came from that player without changing the underlying damage total.

Critical hit rate and Combat Advantage rate

Critical hit rate is the percentage of counted hits marked critical. Combat Advantage rate is the percentage of counted hits marked with Combat Advantage in the log.

These rates describe the hits recorded in the selected scope. Strikeglass does not invent game state that is missing from the log.
